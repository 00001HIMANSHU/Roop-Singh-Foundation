BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace contact@roopsinghfoundation.org with the foundation's real monitored email.
2. Replace the placeholder phone number and location with approved public contact details.
3. Add the exact legal name, registration type/number, registered address and governing-body information only after verification.
4. Do not claim 12A/80G, CSR-1, FCRA, government recognition or tax-deductibility unless the relevant approval/registration is actually held.
5. Add authorised bank/UPI payment details only after verifying the receiving account is in the foundation's authorised name. The current donation page is an enquiry form, not a payment gateway.
6. Replace sample stock photos with genuine activity photographs only when you have permission and informed consent. Take extra care with children's privacy.
7. The contact, volunteer and donation forms currently use mailto. They open the visitor's email app and do not store submissions. For reliable online submissions, connect Web3Forms, Formspree or your own backend and add spam protection.
8. Test all pages on mobile and desktop before publishing.
