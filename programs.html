Where we focus

Practical support. Meaningful possibilities.

Our programme areas are designed around everyday needs that can make a real difference to children and families.

EDUCATION

Learning support

Encouraging children to continue learning through educational guidance, learning activities and access to supportive study environments.

EDUCATIONAL SUPPLIES

School essentials

Helping children access notebooks, pens, pencils, erasers, sharpeners, school bags and other essential learning materials.

COMMUNITY WELFARE

Winter care

Supporting people in need with seasonal essentials such as blankets, sweaters and warm clothing during colder months.
