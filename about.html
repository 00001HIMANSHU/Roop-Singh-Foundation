Who we are

Compassion in action. Opportunity for all.

Roop Singh Foundation is being built around a simple belief: every person deserves dignity, and every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and build a better future.

Our story

Rooted in care. Focused on lasting change.

Our purpose is to respond to real needs in our communities, beginning with education and essential support for children and families. We aim to work responsibly, transparently and in partnership with people who share this vision.

As our work grows, we will share programme updates, activities and verified impact information here so supporters can understand how contributions are used.

OUR INSPIRATION & LEGACY

A Foundation Built on Values, Inspired by a Legacy

ROOP SINGH FOUNDATION is a heartfelt tribute to Roop Singh Ji, whose name inspires our commitment to humanity, education and social service.

We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, every family deserves dignity, and every person deserves kindness and support.

Through this foundation, we aim to carry forward a meaningful legacy by supporting underprivileged children, promoting education and helping families in need.

For us, this is more than an organisation. It is a promise to make a positive difference in people's lives and keep the spirit of service alive.
